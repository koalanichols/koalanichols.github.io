Agenda Edu Automação

A personal tool that turns school notification emails into calendar events and short daily and weekly summaries.

What it does

The school sends its announcements through the Agenda Edu platform by email. This tool reads only the messages the owner has labeled AgendaEdu in Gmail, then:

  • extracts the student, the category, the summary, and any dates or deadlines;
  • creates Google Calendar events, with reminders one week and one day ahead, for items that have a confirmed date;
  • sends the owner a summary each day and a recap each week, by email and by WhatsApp.

Who uses it

It is a private tool built and used by one person, its developer, for their own family. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Google permissions

The tool asks for three Google permissions, each for one purpose:

  • Read Gmail messages (gmail.readonly), to read the labeled school emails.
  • Send Gmail messages (gmail.send), to email the summaries to the owner.
  • Manage Google Calendar events (calendar), to add the school events to the owner’s calendar.

See the Privacy Policy for how this data is handled.
